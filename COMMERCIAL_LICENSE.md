# Request a commercial license

SoloRoleplayer is free for noncommercial use. To use the app or its code to earn money, request a license first.

Email **support@wordblocklabs.com** with:
1. What you want to make or run (a product, a hosted service, paid sessions, something else).
2. How you will charge, and roughly how many people it will reach.

Small-scale uses are granted free on request. Larger uses are priced case by case. No commercial use is licensed until it is agreed in writing.
